About

Background / Interests

I'm a software engineer at Respawn Entertainment. I started there as an intern and came back full-time after finishing a custom Game Development degree.

Most of my favorite work happens where code meets sound. I like building a gameplay system and then the audio logic that reacts to it: occlusion that follows the walls, reverb that knows what room you're in, music that drives the lights.

Before Respawn, I led Resonance, a 12-person multiplayer arena shooter, as project lead, audio lead, and gameplay programmer.

What I'm working on

  • Growing my technical sound design and audio systems skills
  • Studying how sound works differently across genres, starting with magic versus shooters
  • Putting together an audio demo reel

Outside of work

I play piano, mostly improvising. I also do archery, watch a lot of anime, collect Pokémon cards, and go out of my way for good food.
