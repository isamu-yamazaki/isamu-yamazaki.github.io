Resonance

Unity / C# / PurrNet / Wwise / HLSL

A multiplayer arena shooter made by a 12-person team with Chapman University and the University of Waterloo. I was project lead, audio lead, and a gameplay programmer.

  • Networked gameplay: a spline-driven train with working doors, a zipline you can jump off, a sonar disc that sticks to walls and respects line of sight, and directional damage indicators
  • Wwise systems that read the level geometry to handle occlusion, reverb, and directional ambience
  • URP shaders in HLSL, including a refracting bubble shield, a glitch death effect, and volumetric fog
